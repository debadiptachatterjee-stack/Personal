How I work

I start with the smallest version that proves the idea, then spend the remaining time on the details people feel but cannot name: spacing, easing, the pause before a screen loads.

I design in the browser and write production code, so what you approve is what ships. No hand-off gap, no lost intent.

Outside client work I maintain Kiln, write about interface craft, and photograph architecture on weekends.
